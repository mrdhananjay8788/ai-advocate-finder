# AI Chatbot Safety & Compliance

This platform utilizes a Large Language Model (LLM) via the OpenAI API to facilitate the initial client intake process. Because legal matters are sensitive, strict safety bounds are designed into the prompt architecture.

## Permitted AI Actions
The AI Chatbot is strictly instructed to:
- Collect factual case information from the client.
- Ask clarifying intake questions (e.g., location, timeline, parties involved).
- Structure unstructured chat text into predefined categories (`CaseType`, `UrgencyLevel`, `Jurisdiction`) for database storage.
- Support the advocate matching engine by providing clear parameters.

## Restricted AI Actions
The AI Chatbot is explicitly instructed and technically constrained so that it must **NOT**:
- Provide legal advice or interpretation of the law.
- Tell users what specific legal action they should or should not take.
- Guarantee any legal outcomes.
- Predict court decisions or rulings.
- Claim a user will win or lose a case.

## Disclaimer
> **IMPORTANT:** The Advocate Finder platform and its AI intake system do not replace a qualified human legal professional. The output generated by the AI is for intake classification only and does not constitute attorney-client privilege or formal legal counsel. 

*Note: This platform has not been audited for formal legal compliance certification (e.g., GDPR, HIPAA, or local Bar Association advertising rules). It is currently a Minimum Viable Product (Phase 1) for demonstration purposes.*
